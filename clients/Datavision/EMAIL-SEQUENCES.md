## COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

### CAN-SPAM (US) — Applies if sending to US addresses

- ✅ **From name:** Real person (e.g., "María García, Datavision")
- ✅ **Subject line:** Not deceptive (no false re: lines in Email 2)
- ✅ **Physical address:** Include in footer: "Datavision, [MEXICO ADDRESS]"
- ✅ **Unsubscribe link:** Functional, removes within 10 days
- ✅ **Opt-in records:** Maintain documentation of explicit consent

⚠️ **Note:** Current sequence uses "Re:" in Email 2 subject. This is borderline. Options:
- **Safe:** Change to "Partner del Año Microsoft — auditoría de Azure"
- **Or:** Add disclaimer "(nuevo email)" after the Re:

### GDPR (EU) — Applies if sending to EU addresses

- ✅ **Consent:** Document explicit opt-in (not applicable if Mexico-only warm list)
- ✅ **Data processing:** Clarify if email platform processes data in EU
- ✅ **Privacy link:** Include link to Datavision privacy policy
- ✅ **Right to deletion:** Honor unsubscribe within 30 days

⚠️ **Recommendation:** If list includes EU contacts, ensure email platform is GDPR-compliant (e.g., HubSpot, Klaviyo, Mailchimp with EU data processing addendum).

### CASL (Canada) — Applies if sending to Canadian addresses

- ✅ **Consent:** Express/implied consent must be documented
- ✅ **Sender ID:** Real name + company (e.g., "María García, Datavision")
- ✅ **Physical address:** Required
- ✅ **Unsubscribe:** One-click unsubscribe required

⚠️ **Recommendation:** Canadian recipients should have prior business relationship (implied consent valid for 24 months).

### Mexico Best Practices (No legal requirement, but recommended)

- ✅ **Local language:** Spanish (current)
- ✅ **Local numbers:** Include Mexico phone number in footer
- ✅ **Respect no-contact:** Honor DNCR (National Do Not Call Registry) if applicable
- ✅ **Unsubscribe:** Always include (builds trust even if not legally required)

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Email Service Provider (ESP) Setup

**Recommended ESPs for this campaign:**
1. **HubSpot** (best for B2B + CRM integration)
2. **Klaviyo** (strong automation + analytics)
3. **Mailchimp** (simple, affordable, good for starting)
4. **ActiveCampaign** (robust for Mexico + LatAm)

**Key features needed:**
- Automation workflows (send Email 2 if Email 1 opened, etc.)
- UTM parameter tracking (to link emails → calendar bookings)
- Deliverability monitoring (bounce, spam complaint tracking)
- A/B testing interface (subject line, send time)
- Integration with calendar tool (Calendly, Acuity, etc.)

### Workflow Setup Example (HubSpot)

```
1. Upload list to HubSpot
   - Tag: "Azure Audit Prospect"
   - Exclude: "Datavision Customer"

2. Create automation:
   - Trigger: Contact added to list
   - Email 1 sends immediately
   - Wait 3 days
   - Email 2 sends (if Email 1 was opened)
   - Wait 4 days
   - Email 3 sends (if no click from Email 1-2)
   - Wait 6 days
   - Email 4 sends (time-sensitive, reset timing)
   - Wait 7 days
   - Email 5 sends (final touch)

3. A/B tests:
   - Split Email 1 subject line at 50% | 50%
   - Winning version carries to subsequent emails
   - Measure: open rate, click rate

4. Sync with calendar:
   - Calendly embed in CTA links
   - HubSpot logs meeting bookings to contact record
   - Sales can use contact history to personalize audit call
```
